Hover or focus either yearly point to see both daily averages, observation totals, and calendar-day counts. Every day belongs to exactly one group, including days with zero recorded observations.

Holidays are distinct dates marked as actual holidays in the existing calendar. Observed-only substitute dates belong to the non-holiday group. Leap years have 366 days; other years have 365.

These are species-category observation records, not individual birds, unique checklists, or population estimates. Unavailable or inconsistent summaries produce gaps rather than zeros. The complete-year range is 2012–2025.
